Official Rules & Calculation Formula

1. Official Reference: AYUSH & AYUSH Education Section, Office Memorandum No. 2445/XL-1/2020-08/2012, dated 13 October 2021 (Provisional Seniority List S.No. 246 to 753).

2. Superannuation Date Determination (Fundamental Rule 56):

=IF(LEFT(DOB, 2) = "01",
  EOMONTH(DATE(Year + Superannuation_Age, Month, 1), -1),
  EOMONTH(DATE(Year + Superannuation_Age, Month, 1), 0))
  • Rule 1 (1st day of month): If date of birth falls on the 1st day of a month, the officer superannuates on the afternoon of the last day of the preceding month upon attaining retirement age.
  • Rule 2 (Other dates): If date of birth falls on the 2nd day or later, the officer superannuates on the afternoon of the last day of that same birth month upon attaining retirement age.
